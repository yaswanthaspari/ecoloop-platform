Why Responsible E-Waste Matters

Electronic waste can contain hazardous materials that may cause environmental harm when disposed of improperly. Responsible recovery extends product life and can reduce the demand for new resource extraction.

  • Reuse keeps working items productive
  • Repair can extend useful life significantly
  • Recycling may recover valuable materials
  • Approved collection prevents hazardous exposure
