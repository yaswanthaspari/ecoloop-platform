About the Project

Empowering Responsible Electronics Recovery

EcoLoop is an end-to-end circular platform engineered to combat e-waste through AI category detection, deterministic recovery decision-making, and verified environmental education.

AI-Assisted Vision

Deep learning classifier powered by MobileNetV2 for automated device identification, with resilient manual fallback.

Deterministic Engine

Multi-tier rule engine assessing repair economics, physical condition, and critical safety hazards like battery swelling.

EcoImpact Knowledge

Authoritative, peer-reviewed knowledge repository citing EPA, UNEP, and Basel Action Network on environmental hazards.

The 5 Circular Recovery Pathways

1. Direct Reuse & Resale

Functional electronics in good condition are kept in circulation, avoiding new resource extraction.

2. Repair & Restoration

Devices with minor defects where repair costs do not exceed economic feasibility thresholds.

3. Refurbishment

Complete hardware overhauls, testing, and component cleaning for commercial secondary markets.

4. Parts Recovery / Harvesting

Extracting viable displays, RAM, power ICs, and casing from irreparable devices to fix others.

5. Certified Material Recycling

Safe extraction of precious metals and proper neutralizing of toxic substances through licensed e-waste processors.

Academic & Environmental Prototype

Developed as part of the Solid Waste Management (SWM) curriculum to demonstrate how automated workflows and AI can support circular economic transitions and reduce municipal electronic waste.
